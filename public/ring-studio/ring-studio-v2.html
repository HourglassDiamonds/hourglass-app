Custom Design Process

A calm, guided process
from first direction to final ring.

Each project is refined slowly and clearly so the final piece feels personal, elegant, and thoroughly considered.

01

Begin with the conversation

Shape preference, finger coverage, lifestyle, and the feeling the ring should carry all come first.

02

Refine the setting language

We study profile, silhouette, and stone relationship so the design feels intentional from every angle.

03

Review stone and material

Center stone character, side stone balance, and metal tone are considered together as one composition.

04

Complete with restraint

The final ring is finished with close attention to proportion, polish, and quieter detail.
