## 1. Executive summary

The Diamond Guide cut / sparkle / light performance cluster spans **two category taxonomies** — `Diamond Cut` (11 articles) and `Light Performance` (10 articles) — plus **four certification/buying bridge articles** with substantial cut/light content. **21 core articles** map directly to this topical cluster. No dedicated articles exist today for Hearts & Arrows, Triple Excellent, or AGS Ideal as standalone topics (those concepts appear only inside certification and proportion copy).

**Recommended flagship:** `what-is-diamond-cut` — strongest Hourglass advisor voice in the cluster (~900 words), positioned first on the `/diamond-guide/diamond-cut` hub “Begin here” row, highest cut-specific inbound links (12), and mirrors the `what-is-diamond-clarity` / `what-is-diamond-fluorescence` consolidation pattern.

**Recommended long-term structure (12 surviving URLs + bridges):**

| Role | Count | Slugs |
|------|------:|-------|
| **Flagship** | 1 | `what-is-diamond-cut` |

## 4. Flagship recommendation

### Primary: `what-is-diamond-cut`

**Why this slug wins over alternatives:**

| Criterion | `what-is-diamond-cut` | `diamond-sparkle-explained` | `how-diamond-cut-affects-light-performance` |
|-----------|----------------------|----------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| **Breadth** | Cut definition, grades, proportions, performance thesis, buyer mistakes | Sparkle vocabulary only | Performance mechanics only |
| **Search intent** | “What is diamond cut” — top-of-funnel definitional | “Diamond sparkle” — colloquial, narrower | Long-tail, mid-funnel |
| **Authority potential** | Highest — mirrors clarity/fluorescence flagship pattern | Strong but consumer-language, not grading authority | Generic educational tone |
| **User value** | Explains why two Excellent rounds differ; Hourglass voice | Good B/F/S umbrella | Redundant with flagship after consolidation |
| **Internal linking** | 12 inbound; cut hub “begin here” #1 | 9 inbound; light hub “most read” | 7 inbound |
| **Visual opportunities** | GIA cut scale, light-path diagram, B/F/S summary chart | B/F/S comparison graphic | Light-path / proportion diagram |
