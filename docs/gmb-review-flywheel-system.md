# GMB Review Flywheel — Hourglass Diamonds

Internal planning document for Google Business Profile reviews, local authority, and the compounding trust loop that supports Charlotte visibility and consultation quality.

---

## 1. Purpose

Google reviews, client photos, owner replies, and consistent Google Business Profile (GMB) activity form a **compounding local SEO and trust engine** for Hourglass Diamonds.

Each positive cycle strengthens:

- **Local pack visibility** — relevance, prominence, and engagement signals Google uses for map and local search
- **Pre-consultation trust** — prospects who discover us via search, maps, or referral often read reviews before reaching out
- **Brand proof** — specific stories about guidance, custom design, and outcomes reinforce the same positioning as the site and Diamond Studio
- **Long-term Charlotte authority** — sustained review velocity and quality matter more than one-time spikes

This system is **process and documentation only**. Execution happens in Google Business Profile, client communication, and weekly ops—not in product code.

---

## 2. Weekly Objectives

Each week, prioritize:

| Objective | Why it matters |
|-----------|----------------|
| **Maintain consistent review velocity** | Steady new reviews signal an active, trusted business—not a dormant listing |
| **Increase photo reviews** | Visual proof (rings, moments, hands) lifts engagement and authenticity |
| **Increase review quality / detail** | Specific stories rank better emotionally and algorithmically than one-line generics |
| **Improve local engagement signals** | Replies, posts, photos, and Q&A activity reinforce profile freshness |
| **Build long-term Charlotte authority** | Local jewelry is relationship-driven; GMB is the public ledger of that reputation |

---

## 3. Review Request Timing

Request reviews only after a **genuine positive milestone**—never mass-blast or incentivize.

| Moment | Notes |
|--------|--------|
| **After successful delivery** | Ring or piece received; client has had time to see it in real life (a few days post-delivery is often ideal) |
| **After proposal success** | Partner said yes; emotion is high—ask while memory is vivid, with sensitivity to privacy |
| **After meaningful consultation experience** | Client felt heard and informed even if purchase is later; only if the conversation was clearly valued |
| **After custom redesign completion** | Heirloom, reset, or redesign delivered—stories here often mention process and trust |

**Do not request** when the experience was unresolved, rushed, or still in active issue resolution.

---

## 4. Preferred Review Style

Reviews should read like a **real client voice**, not marketing copy.

**Aim for:**

- Natural, conversational tone
- Specific details (shape, setting, what surprised them, how Justin / the team helped)
- Emotionally genuine outcome (confidence, relief, joy—not hype)
- Honest length (a few thoughtful sentences beat a single adjective)

**Avoid:**

- Incentivized or quid-pro-quo language (“in exchange for…”)
- Robotic templates clients were asked to paste
- SEO-stuffed keywords (“best jeweler Charlotte NC engagement rings discount”)
- Generic praise with no story (“Great service, 5 stars.”)

**Optional gentle prompt for clients:**  
*“If you’re open to it, mentioning what we worked on together—or sharing a photo—helps other couples find us.”*

---

## 5. High-Value Review Elements

Prioritize reviews that include one or more of:

| Element | Value |
|---------|--------|
| **Client photos** | Ring on hand, proposal moment, detail shots—strongest social and GMB proof |
| **Proposal stories** | Narrative arc: search → guidance → yes moment |
| **Mention of guidance / process** | Aligns with Hourglass positioning (calm, personal, expert) |
| **Trust language** | Felt heard, no pressure, transparent, patient |
| **Custom design experience** | Redesign, heirloom, one-of-one—not only off-the-shelf |
| **Emotional outcome** | Confidence, “exactly what I wanted,” family reaction |

When replying publicly, **mirror** these elements briefly so future readers see the same themes.

---

## 6. Weekly GMB Activities

Minimum weekly rhythm on the Google Business Profile:

| Activity | Cadence |
|----------|---------|
| **Reply to all reviews** | Every new review within a few business days; thank specifics, stay warm and professional |
| **Upload new photos** | Fresh work, studio, details, or client-approved images (with permission) |
| **Publish one GMB post weekly** | One post: educational tip, completed piece, seasonal note, or local trust angle |
| **Monitor Q&A** | Answer new questions; add proactive FAQs if the same questions repeat |
| **Monitor ranking movement** | Local pack / map visibility for core terms (Charlotte, engagement rings, custom jeweler, etc.) |
| **Monitor calls / directions / messages** | Note volume trends; correlate with posts, reviews, or seasonality |
